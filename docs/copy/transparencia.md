### Cómo valoro, qué se marca y de qué vivo

**Ninguna vía de ingreso compra una reseña.** Ni un rocódromo, ni una marca, ni un programa de afiliación. Si un día esto cambia, lo diré aquí y en cada reseña afectada.

**Cómo valoro**

- Visito los rocódromos y puntúo cada criterio de 1 a 5. La nota del autor de una ficha es la media de mis notas en los criterios activos. Es mi opinión, y la firmo como tal.
- En los pies de gato y los resoladores, puntúo solo si los he usado. Si no, lo digo («No los he usado») y enseño los datos del fabricante y el precio.
- [XAVI: cómo es una visita tuya, cuántas veces vas, cuánto tiempo estás y qué compruebas cada vez.]
- [XAVI: qué no mides o no puedes valorar y lo dices.]

**Qué se calcula y qué se opina**

Los índices no son opinión: se calculan igual para todos los rocódromos con los datos de cada ficha.

- **Coste mensual a 2 visitas por semana.** Cuenta unas 8,67 visitas al mes (2 × 52 / 12). Es el menor entre la cuota mensual, las entradas de día y el bono de 10 entradas, repartido a esas visitas. La matrícula se reparte en 12 meses y se suma solo a la cuota mensual.
- **Índice de precio (1–5, 5 es el más barato).** Sobre ese coste mensual, con tramos fijos de 7,50 €: 5 por debajo de 50 €, 4 de 50 a 57,49 €, 3 de 57,50 a 64,99 €, 2 de 65 a 72,49 € y 1 desde 72,50 €. Mientras haya menos de 10 rocódromos con precio publicado se usan estos tramos; con 10 o más se pasa a quintiles. Revisaré los tramos cada octubre. - **Índice principiante (1–5).** Media ponderada: mi nota en «para principiantes» (50 %), si hay cursos de iniciación (20 %), si alquilan material (15 %) y si se puede empezar sin saber asegurar, porque hay bloque o no exigen test (15 %). Lo que no sé cuenta cero, y lo indico.
- **Índice familia (1–5).** Zona infantil (40 %), edad mínima de seis años o menos (20 %), cafetería (20 %) y mi nota en «para familias» si existe (20 %).
- **Nota de lectores.** Es la media de la nota global de las opiniones visibles. Solo se muestra cuando hay al menos 3 opiniones; hasta entonces ves cuántas hay, sin media.
- **Grados y estilo no son notas.** Sirven para elegir: si los grados son generosos o duros frente a la media de Barcelona y si el estilo es técnico o dinámico. Ninguno es mejor y no entran en ninguna media ni índice.

## EN

### How I rate, what is flagged and how this is funded

**No source of income buys a review.** Not a climbing gym, not a brand, not an affiliate programme. If that ever changes, I will say so here and on every review affected.

**How I rate**

- I visit the gyms and score each criterion from 1 to 5. A listing's author score is the average of my scores across the active criteria. It is my opinion, and I sign it as such.
- For climbing shoes and resolers, I only score what I have used. If I haven't, I say so ("I haven't used them") and show the manufacturer's data and the checked price.
- [XAVI: what one of your visits looks like, how often you go, how long you stay and what you check each time.]
- [XAVI: what you do not measure or cannot judge, and say so.]

**What is calculated and what is opinion**

The indices are not opinion: they are calculated the same way for every gym, from the data on its listing.

- **Monthly cost at 2 visits a week.** It assumes about 8.67 visits a month (2 × 52 / 12). It takes the lowest of the monthly membership, day entries and the 10-entry pass, spread over those visits. The joining fee is spread over 12 months and added only to the monthly membership.
- **Price index (1–5, 5 is cheapest).** Based on that monthly cost, in fixed bands of €7.50: 5 under €50, 4 from €50 to €57.49, 3 from €57.50 to €64.99, 2 from €65 to €72.49 and 1 from €72.50. While fewer than 10 gyms have a published price, these bands apply; from 10 on, quintiles. I will review the bands each October. [XAVI: confirm you want to publish the exact bands.]
- **Beginner index (1–5).** A weighted average: my score for "beginner-friendly" (50%), whether there are intro courses (20%), whether they rent gear (15%) and whether you can start without knowing how to belay, because there is bouldering or no test is required (15%). What I don't know counts as zero, and I say so.
- **Family index (1–5).** Kids' area (40%), minimum age of six or under (20%), café (20%) and my "family-friendly" score if there is one (20%).
- **Readers' score.** The average of the overall score of visible reviews. It only shows when there are at least 3 reviews; until then you see how many there are, without an average.
- **Grading and style are not scores.** They help you choose: whether grades are generous or hard compared with Barcelona's average, and whether the style is technical or dynamic. Neither is better, and neither enters any average or index.

**Data and dates**

Every price carries the date I checked it by hand on the gym's website. If a long time has passed, the listing says "may have changed". Climbing shoe prices are given as a range and also carry a review date. You can help: if you spot an outdated detail, write to me.

**Invited visits**

Sometimes a gym, a workshop or a brand invites me in or lends me gear. When that happens, the review says so and carries the "Invited visit" flag. My opinion is not negotiated and not reviewed by them before I publish it. [XAVI: confirm there is no prior review by whoever invites you.]

**Affiliate links**

Some links for buying climbing shoes are affiliate links: if you buy through them, I may earn a commission, and it costs you nothing extra. They carry the "Affiliate link" or "affiliate" flag and the listing says it contains them. Models without a link are compared just the same and do not score lower. Ranking and scores do not depend on whether there is a commission. [XAVI: name the programmes you use, for example those on the listings, and confirm there are no other commercial agreements.]

**Reader reviews**

They are published straight away and I check them afterwards. I only hide those that break the terms (insults, advertising, other people's personal data, unlawful content), never for being negative. [XAVI: confirm this criterion and whether you would widen it.]

**If I get it wrong**
